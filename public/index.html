openmeet

Open-source group video calling. A WebRTC SFU you can read.

What works

  • Real SFU — each peer uploads once, the server fans out to everyone else.
  • Audio + video + screen share.
  • Mute, camera off, hangup.
  • Multiple simultaneous rooms.
  • Read every line of the code in an afternoon.

What doesn't (yet)

  • No TURN — ~15% of users behind strict NATs won't connect.
  • No recording, no breakout rooms, no chat.
  • No authentication — anyone with the URL joins.
  • 1-on-1 quality is best; 4+ peers may drop frames on a laptop SFU.
